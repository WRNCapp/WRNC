Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This policy explains how WRNC, operated by Swear Like A Sailor, LLC, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the WRNC app and website.

Information you provide

We collect the email address and authentication information needed to create and secure your account. We also store the vehicle details, activity records, notes, mileage, photos, receipts, documents, and other build records that you choose to add to WRNC.

How we use information

We use this information to operate WRNC, display your vehicle history and Build Passport, secure your account, respond to support requests, diagnose problems, and improve the service. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers and analytics

WRNC uses service providers for authentication, database hosting, private file storage, application hosting, and website analytics. These providers process information to provide their services to WRNC and are subject to their own security and privacy obligations.

Our public website may collect limited technical and referral information, such as pages visited, browser type, referring site, and campaign parameters. The mobile app does not include advertising SDKs. WRNC is auditing the exact production telemetry used by the V1 mobile binary and will keep this policy and App Store privacy disclosures aligned with the shipping implementation.

Storage and security

WRNC uses Supabase for production authentication, database, and file-storage functions. Vehicle records and uploaded files use access controls designed to restrict them to the authenticated account owner. No system is completely secure, so keep your password private and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

Retention, access, and deletion

We retain account information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide WRNC, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, maintain security and backups, and enforce agreements. WRNC is implementing an in-app account-deletion workflow for the App Store release path. Until that workflow is available in the shipping app, contact support@wrnc.app for deletion questions or assistance. Some information may be retained where required by law or necessary for security, dispute resolution, or backup integrity.

Your choices

You choose what vehicle and build information you add to WRNC. You may contact support@wrnc.app regarding access, correction, or deletion questions. Device permissions for photos, files, and similar resources can be managed through your operating system where supported.

Children and changes

WRNC is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We may update this policy as WRNC changes. The revised policy will be posted here with a new effective date.

Questions? support@wrnc.app
