WRNC Support

Get help with your WRNC account, vehicles, activities, documents, or Build Passport.

Contact support

Email support@wrnc.app with the email address associated with your account, a short description of the issue, and screenshots when helpful. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment details, or sensitive vehicle documents by email.

We aim to acknowledge support requests within two business days.

Account and data requests

WRNC is implementing an in-app account-deletion workflow for the App Store release path. Until that workflow is available in the shipping app, email support@wrnc.app from the address associated with your account for deletion questions or assistance. The Privacy Policy explains retention and deletion practices.

Before contacting us

Confirm that you are using the latest version of WRNC and that your device has an internet connection. Include your device model, iOS version, and the action you were attempting when reporting a technical problem.
